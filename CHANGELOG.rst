0.1.0
-------

* First version: support graphs of timber frames
  read from JSON, drawn after Bruun et al. 2024, in Rhino, on the Grasshopper canvas,
  as SVG and as PDF. Graphviz is no longer used.
* Drawing of any COMPAS graph, a layered layout for directed graphs such as sequences of actions,
  and ``from_model`` for compas_model models that carry the state of an intervention on their graph.
* Documentation with MkDocs, published to GitHub Pages.
