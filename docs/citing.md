# Citing

Graph Drawing is developed at the
[Professorship of Digital Fabrication](https://www.arc.ed.tum.de/df),
Technical University of Munich.

If you use it for your work, please cite it with:

```latex
@misc{graph_drawing,
    title={{Graph Drawing}: schematic drawings of graphs for Rhino, Grasshopper, SVG and PDF},
    author={Rein, Tizian},
    howpublished={https://github.com/augmentedfabricationlab/graph_drawing},
    note={Professorship of Digital Fabrication, Technical University of Munich},
    year={2026},
}
```

The support graphs follow the support hierarchy graph of Bruun et al. 2024,
*Construction Robotics* 8:20, Fig. 7.
